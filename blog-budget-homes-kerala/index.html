How to plan a budget-friendly home in Kerala.

Published by Anon Homes — builders in Attingal

Building a home in Kerala is a dream for many. With the right planning, you can build a beautiful, budget-friendly home without compromising on quality or looks. These are the decisions that matter most.

  1. 01

    Choose the right plot

    The groundwork of a budget home starts with the land. Look for a plot that doesn't need extensive levelling or soil replacement — a flat plot can save you a significant amount on initial groundwork.

  2. 02

    Keep the design simple

    Complex forms, extra corners and irregular shapes all push up construction costs. A clean rectangular or square plan is more cost-effective, and pairs naturally with minimal interiors.

  3. 03

    Pick cost-effective materials

    Work with your civil contractor to choose durable, locally available materials. Laterite stone, plentiful in Kerala, is a strong choice. For floors, good vitrified tiles do the job of far more expensive marble.

  4. 04

    Use natural light and air

    Kerala's tropical climate is ideal for daylight and ventilation. Large windows and proper cross-ventilation cut your long-term electricity bill for lighting and cooling.

  5. 05

    Hire the right builders

    Experienced builders keep a project inside its budget. At Anon Homes we work with transparent pricing and tight project management, so there are no surprise overruns along the way. See how we approach budget homes and our other services.
