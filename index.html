About usAttingal, Kerala

A personal connection to design.

Anon Homes is a construction and interior design company in Attingal, Trivandrum, and we go beyond design — delivering luxurious, innovative homes, interiors and commercial buildings that answer directly to what each client needs.

Our first priority is time — understanding how you live, then carrying that through every stage of planning, building and finishing.

  1. Stage 01

    Concept

    Every project starts with a concept, and every concept comes from a personal connection with the client.

  2. Stage 02

    Collaboration

    We take the time to understand what you need, and every member of the Anon team carries it through the work.

  3. Stage 03

    Create

    An authentic style and a home identity our clients will cherish for a lifetime.
